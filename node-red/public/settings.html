These values are stored in Docker's persistent /data volume. The same complete settings editor is also available from LAYERS → SETTINGS on the main display.

Station

Southern Hemisphere latitudes must be negative (for example, Wellington is about -41.3). Longitudes east of Greenwich are positive.

City clocks

Up to eight clocks. Use IANA timezones such as Europe/London or Asia/Tokyo.

Weather

Map data

The built-in carrier source uses only coarse, publicly reported USNI Fleet Tracker regions—not precise live military positions.

DX Spot Source

Choose Telnet cluster for W3LPL, DXSpider, AR-Cluster and similar nodes. Choose DX Summit to poll its web/API feed. DX Summit polling is clamped to at least 60 seconds.
